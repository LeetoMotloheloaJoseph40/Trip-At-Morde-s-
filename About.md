Who it's for

Everyone from '06 and beyond. My friends. The people I meet every day, whether we talk for a year or for five minutes at a bus stop. A few of you are in the track titles. Most of you are in the sound.

Why it's on GitHub

Because a repo is how I keep things I care about: versioned, backed up, and open. The audio is here to be heard. The stems are here so the tracks can be opened up and understood, the way you read someone's source code to see how it works. The artwork lives next to them because it belongs to them.
