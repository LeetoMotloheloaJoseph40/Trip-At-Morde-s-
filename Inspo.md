Inspiration
The park and the blue jay

My nickname comes from Mordecai, the blue jay in Regular Show, and ThePark is where that show lives. That cartoon is the spine of this project. Look at the titles:

Anti Pops! and Pops At It ! play on the villain and the sweet old landlord.
Rigby A Rockstar (intro) and Rigby and Rabonas are for the best friend who is always in the middle of the trouble.
The Crush is the song every Mordecai eventually writes.
Trip At Morde's and At Morde's are the same idea: a visit, a hangout, a place you end up.

The show is about two friends in a dead-end job who keep stumbling into something enormous. Making music next to everything else I do feels a bit like that.

The people

My Friend Kim From Tokyo, My Guys (0600) and Who ! are about the people around me. Everyone I meet in a day leaves a line or a mood somewhere in the record.

The names I'm tipping my hat to

Some titles name artists directly: SZA @ThePark and Trippie @Morde's imagine them walking into my world. They are tributes. I am not claiming their music.

The look

The spaced-out lettering, M O R D E C A I, is my signature. It slows the name down so it reads like someone saying it out loud.
